Everything You Need to Write

Powerful tools designed specifically for novel writers

🤖

AI Writing Assistant

22+ AI writing modes: Continue, Expand, Summarize, Rewrite, Dialogue, Description, Action, Emotion, Analyze, Brainstorm, Outline, World-Build, Character Bio, Backstory, Plot Twist, and more. Works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama & LM Studio.

📋

Genre Templates

Jump-start your story with 8 pre-built templates: Fantasy Hero's Journey, Romance, Thriller, Sci-Fi, Mystery, Horror, Literary Fiction. Each template includes detailed story beats.

✏️

Rich Text Editor

Full formatting with TipTap editor. Split view, Focus Mode, Typewriter Mode for distraction-free writing.

📤

Export Options

Export to PDF, DOCX, EPUB, TXT, JSON, and HTML. Professional document export for manuscripts and eBooks.

🏁

NaNoWriMo Mode

Built-in writing goal tracker with 50K target. Daily progress tracking, words-per-day calculator, and session goals.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export and import your entire workspace. Full project backup to JSON for safekeeping.

📊

Story Planning

Visual matrix view with drag-and-drop. Track Goals, Conflict, and Outcome for each scene.

📖

World Codex

Characters with deep profiles, Locations, Items, Lore, and Events. Build your entire universe.

🔗

Character Relationships

Visual relationship map showing connections between characters with types and strength.

📅

Timeline

Chronological story events with visual timeline. Year-grouped view with click-to-edit.

Tag Management

Custom colored tags to organize scenes, characters, and notes. Quick filtering.

📚

Research Notes

Keep all your research in one place. Categorize by topic, search and filter easily.

📈

Writing Analysis

Local text analysis - no API needed! Word stats, readability, dialogue ratio, sentiment, story structure.

📜

Revision History

Automatic version tracking. Browse past versions and restore any previous version.

💬

AI Chat

Ask questions about your story, characters, or plot. Get writing advice and brainstorming help.

🎯

Writing Sprints

Timed writing sessions with word count goals. Stay focused and track your progress.

🎨

Themes

Dark, Light, and Sepia modes. Easy on the eyes for long writing sessions.

💾

Auto-Save

Your work is automatically saved. Never lose a word with local storage.

Your Data Stays Yours

All data stored locally on your computer. No cloud, no subscription, no tracking.

How It Works

Get started in minutes

1

Download & Install

Download the .exe file from GitHub Releases. Double-click to install - it's that simple!

2

Create Your Project

3

Start Writing

4

Build Your World

Add characters, locations, and lore in Codex. Plan your story in the Matrix view.

5

Export & Share

Export your finished novel in TXT, JSON, or HTML format. Share with the world!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is Novel Studio really free?
Yes! Novel Studio is 100% free and open source. No hidden fees, no subscription, no premium version. You can download, use, and modify it however you want.
Do I need an API key for AI features?
AI features require an OpenAI or Anthropic API key, which you can get from their websites. However, our Writing Analysis feature works completely offline - no API needed!
Where is my data stored?
All your data is stored locally on your computer. Nothing is sent to the cloud. Your novel, characters, and notes stay private on your machine.
Can I use Novel Studio for commercial projects?
Yes! Novel Studio is licensed under MIT. You can use it for personal or commercial projects without any restrictions.
What platforms does it support?
Currently Novel Studio supports Windows. Mac and Linux versions are planned for future releases.
How do I report bugs or request features?
Head to our GitHub Issues page to report bugs or suggest new features. We welcome contributions from the community!
Can I collaborate with others on a project?
Currently Novel Studio is designed for single-user local editing. You can export your project as JSON and share it with collaborators.
What's the difference between Novel Studio and other writing apps?
Novel Studio combines AI assistance, world-building tools, and writing analysis in one package - all for free. Unlike subscription-based services, you own your data forever.
