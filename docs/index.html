Everything You Need to Write

Powerful tools designed specifically for novel writers

🤖

AI Writing Assistant

12+ AI writing modes including Continue, Expand, Rewrite, Dialogue, and more. Works with OpenAI and Anthropic APIs.

✏️

Rich Text Editor

Full formatting support with TipTap editor. Split view, focus mode, and typewriter mode for distraction-free writing.

📊

Story Planning

Visual matrix view with drag-and-drop chapters and scenes. Track Goals, Conflict, and Outcome for each scene.

📖

World Codex

Build your world with Characters, Locations, Items, Lore, and Events. Everything organized in one place.

🔗

Character Relationships

Visual relationship map showing connections between characters with types and strength indicators.

📅

Timeline

Chronological story events with visual timeline view. Filter by character or location.

🏷️

Tag Management

Custom tags with colors to organize scenes, characters, and notes. Quick filtering across your project.

📈

Writing Analysis

Local text analysis with no API required. Word stats, readability, dialogue ratio, sentiment, and story structure.

📜

Revision History

Automatic version tracking. Browse past versions and restore any previous version with one click.

💬

AI Chat

Ask questions about your story, characters, or plot. Get writing advice and brainstorming help.

🎯

Writing Sprints

Timed writing sessions with word count goals. Stay focused and track your progress.

🎨

Themes

Dark, Light, and Sepia modes. Easy on the eyes for long writing sessions.
